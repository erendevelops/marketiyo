# Long text (experience and story posts)

Where it runs: LinkedIn, Facebook. On LinkedIn the first two lines show before the fold.

## Formats
- `narrative`: short story and takeaway, 120-250 words
- `build-in-public`: process and numbers
- `document`: a 6-10 page document with design notes

## Hooks
- The first two lines show before the fold. Curiosity has to land there.
- Personal experience outperforms general advice.

## Structure
1. The scene, one sentence
2. The tension or problem
3. What you did
4. The takeaway
5. A short question that opens discussion

## Avoid
- Fake rhythm built from excessive line breaks
- Hollow motivational language
