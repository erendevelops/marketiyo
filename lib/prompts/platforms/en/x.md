# Short text (single post or thread)

Where it runs: X, Threads. On X a single post is limited to 280 characters.

## Formats
- `single`: one post, 280 character limit
- `thread`: a run of 4-9 posts
- `quote-take`: a short, sharp counter to an existing view

## Hooks
- The first line has to stand on its own.
- Open with a number, a result, or a clear claim.

## Structure
- Single post: claim, proof, short payoff.
- Thread: one idea per post, the last post carries the call to action.

## Avoid
- Hashtag piles
- Empty openers like "a thread"
