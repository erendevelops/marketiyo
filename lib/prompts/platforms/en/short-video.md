# Short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

Here the viewer is not looking for your product. They are scrolling. If the first two seconds do not stop them, nothing after that matters.

## Formats
- `talking-head`: straight to camera, 20-45 seconds
- `screen-demo`: narrated screen recording, 25-60 seconds
- `before-after`: problem and solution side by side, 15-30 seconds
- `listicle`: fast points, 30-50 seconds

## What the hook is for

A hook does not attract interest, it stops a scroll. Those are different jobs. What stops people is not curiosity but recognition: the viewer sees something from their own life on screen.

Patterns that work:

- Name the situation the viewer lives but has never put words to.
- State the opposite of what is expected, then show it.
- Show the result first and explain how afterwards.
- A concrete moment: a specific hour, a specific number, a specific scene.

## Structure
1. Hook: a familiar situation or a clear claim, no product name
2. Context: one sentence on why it matters
3. Demonstration: on screen or in hand, the proof of the claim
4. Payoff and a single call to action

The product appears at step three at the earliest. Win the viewer first, introduce second.

## Avoid
- Opening with the product name or a feature name
- Intros that begin with a greeting
- Self-introduction, brand history, team tours
- Generic motivational lines
- Update announcements only existing users would understand
- More than one call to action
- Ideas with no visual counterpart. This medium shows, it does not tell.
