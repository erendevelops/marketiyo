# Instagram static

## Formats
- `carousel`: 5-8 slides
- `single`: one image

## Hooks
- Slide one carries a single clear promise.
- On-image text must stay readable, 12 words at most.

## Structure
- Slide 1: hook
- Slides 2-6: one idea per slide
- Final slide: summary and call to action

## Avoid
- Cramming a paragraph onto a slide
- Producing text without any art direction
