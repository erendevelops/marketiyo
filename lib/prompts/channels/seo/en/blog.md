# Blog and search content

## Fields produced
- `title`: the article title. 120 characters at most. Clear enough to earn the click in a search result.
- `primaryKeyword`: the single search phrase this article targets.
- `secondaryKeywords`: phrases the same article naturally covers.
- `searchIntent`: what the searcher wants. Information, comparison, purchase, or a specific brand.
- `funnelStage`: where the reader is. Becoming aware of the problem, weighing options, or deciding.
- `angle`: what separates this article from everything else on the topic.
- `whyItWorks`: one sentence on why this topic makes sense for this product.
- `outlinePoints`: at least 3 main sections. The skeleton of the article.

## What works
- Match the format to the intent. A how-to query wants steps, a comparison query wants a table.
- Pick topics the product is genuinely credible on. Writing about everything dilutes authority rather than building it.
- The article must deliver what the title promises.
- The primary phrase belongs in the title naturally. Forced keyword placement ruins readability.

## Never do this
- Never invent a search volume, difficulty score, or click-through rate. You have no access to that data. Explain intent and stage instead of giving numbers.
- Never imply a ranking guarantee.
- Never propose a synonym of the same topic as a separate article.
- Never propose general topics with no link to the product.
