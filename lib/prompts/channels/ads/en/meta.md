# Meta ads (Facebook, Instagram)

## Fields and limits
- `primaryTexts`: primary text. 125 characters at most. The first line shows before the fold and does the real work.
- `headlines`: headline. 40 characters at most.
- `descriptions`: description. 30 characters at most.
- `visualDirection`: what the image or video shows. One paragraph, concrete at the level of camera and scene.

## What works
- The first sentence of the primary text has to stand on its own.
- A concrete result, a number, or a visible difference. Not an abstract promise.
- Headline and visual should complement each other rather than repeat.
- Each variant tests a different angle. A synonym of the same sentence is not a variant.

## Avoid
- Empty urgency such as "click now" or "do not miss out"
- Health, income, or weight claims that invite rejection
- Second-person lines that imply a personal attribute
- Copy over the character limit. An over-long variant is unusable.
