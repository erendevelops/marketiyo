# Google Search ads (responsive search ad)

## Fields and limits
- `headlines`: at least 3, at most 15. Each 30 characters at most.
- `descriptions`: at least 2, at most 4. Each 90 characters at most.
- `keywordGroups`: keyword clusters. Each has a theme and the keywords belonging to it.

## What works
- The user is searching, not browsing. The copy answers search intent.
- The keyword appears verbatim in at least a few headlines.
- Headlines must be interchangeable, because Google assembles them in different orders.
- Split headlines by role: what it is, the benefit, the difference, the call to action.
- Descriptions carry concrete detail. Price, duration, scope, conditions.

## Avoid
- Repeating the same word in every headline
- Stacks of exclamation marks
- Headlines cut off mid sentence. Each must be complete on its own.
- Copy over the character limit. An over-long variant is unusable.
