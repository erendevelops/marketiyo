# TikTok ads

## Fields and limits
- `adTexts`: ad copy. 100 characters at most.
- `hook`: what is said or shown in the first 2 seconds. One sentence.
- `script`: short script with rough timings, 15 to 30 seconds.

## What works
- An ad that does not look like an ad. Borrow the rhythm of organic video.
- The hook creates tension in the first 2 seconds: an unexpected claim, a visible problem, or a result.
- Show the product, do not describe it. Screen recording or hands-on use is preferred.
- Write the way people speak. The ad copy and the video should sound like one voice.

## Avoid
- Corporate promo film tone
- Scenes that feel like stock footage
- Opening with the brand name. The brand comes last.
- Copy over the character limit. An over-long variant is unusable.
