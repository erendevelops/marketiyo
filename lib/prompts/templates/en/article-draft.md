Turn the given topic into a publishable article draft.

The markdown body must contain:
- Introduction: name the reader's problem in the first paragraph. No long warm-up.
- Main sections: follow the given outline, each with its own heading.
- Concrete examples: stay within what the proof section supports.
- Closing: propose one next step.

Also produce a meta title and a meta description. The meta title is 60 characters at most, the meta description 160 at most.

The primary phrase belongs in the title and the introduction, naturally. Do not sprinkle it through the text.

Never invent a number, percentage, or result. Use only what the proof section contains.
